## Candidate production providers

CHIRPS rainfall, ICPAC/NDMA drought products, Copernicus/Sentinel vegetation, NASA Earthdata, Kenya Open Data/KNBS, county CIDPs/ADPs/budgets, Ministry of Agriculture/KALRO, Water Resources Authority, market feeds and official programme registries. Each connector must record license/terms, publisher, URL, retrieval time, validity time, unit, geography, transformations and confidence before display.

No third-party dataset, imagery asset or model from God’s Eye View is copied into TerraMavuno. A visible map layer is not evidence of redistribution rights.
